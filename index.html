Questions, answered

No surprises.

Is this a subscription?

No. SnipNow is a one-time lifetime purchase with no recurring charges.

How many computers can I activate?

One license can be activated on up to three computers.

Do screenshots leave my computer?

No. Captures are processed and saved locally by the Windows app.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. You enter the license key directly in SnipNow.

Can I keep a screenshot visible?

Yes. Use Pin to desktop to keep a capture above other windows while you work.

Can SnipNow help me move back through folders?

Yes. Double-click an empty area in File Explorer to go back one folder. Double-clicking a file or folder keeps its normal Windows behaviour.
